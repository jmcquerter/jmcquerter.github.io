Privacy Policy — Never Dry Backup

Never Dry Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner to back up his own files to his own Google Drive account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive solely to store and update backup copies of files from his computer.

What it collects and shares

The tool does not collect, store, sell, or share any data with anyone. Data moves only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. No analytics, advertising, or third parties are involved.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" settings.
